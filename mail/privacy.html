Terms & Privacy

This is a personal, open-source email client. It has no company or server behind it.

What the app can access

When you sign in with Google you grant the gmail.modify permission. It lets the app read, send, label and move your email to Trash. It can't permanently delete email.

Where your data goes

  • Your email travels directly between your browser and Google's Gmail API. There is no Mail server, and nothing is sent to anyone else.
  • The app keeps some things in your browser's storage on this device: your sign-in token (valid for about an hour), the list of recently shown emails for offline use, recent searches, unsent drafts and your settings.
  • You can clear this at any time in Settings → Privacy & security, or by signing out.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account, or with Revoke access and sign out in Settings.
